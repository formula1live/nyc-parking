How much does it cost?

Daytime / commuter parking typically runs $20–$35, overnight $37–$60 (Midtown / Times Square is higher) and monthly $500–$1,200+. Metered street parking is $4.50/hr in Manhattan below 96th St and $2.25/hr elsewhere in the city.

Best neighborhoods to try

Harlem / Upper Manhattan offers the most affordable options in Manhattan, while Midtown and Times Square are the priciest. Brooklyn garages near Barclays Center and in Park Slope are often cheaper than Manhattan equivalents, and Queens (Long Island City / Astoria) is worth checking for monthly deals.

Before you drive in

Check alternate side parking rules, event-night surge pricing near Times Square and Madison Square Garden, and whether your garage offers EV charging or monthly discounts before you commit.
